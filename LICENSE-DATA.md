A short credit such as "The Allodium (https://theallodium.org), CC BY 4.0"
is enough for The Allodium's own curation. Please also retain the source
attributions that travel with the authority snapshot (`licenses.json` in
each published dump):

- **Retraction Watch via Crossref**: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
